# Applied Notes – Internal Definition

**Status:** Internal  
**Visibility:** Not published  
**Purpose:** Architectural placeholder

---

## Purpose

Applied Notes translate principles articulated in Working Notes
into a **clearly defined, limited context**.

They are not extensions of Working Notes,
but **contextual applications with explicitly bounded validity**.

Their purpose is not generalization,
but controlled use.

---

## Scope and Constraints

An Applied Note always specifies:

- the concrete context of application  
- the assumptions under which it operates  
- the limits beyond which it does not hold  
- the intended range of effect  

Applied Notes deliberately **do not claim universality**.

They are valid only within the scope they explicitly define.

---

## Relation to Working Notes

Working Notes operate at a **meta level**.

They articulate principles, distinctions, and constraints
that remain valid across contexts,
but are **not directly actionable**.

Applied Notes are downstream of Working Notes.

They assume the principles,
but do not restate or justify them.

Application always requires interpretation.
Applied Notes make this interpretation explicit.

---

## Relation to Exploratory Notes

Exploratory Notes document thinking in motion.

Applied Notes are not exploratory.

They represent a **temporary stabilization**
within a narrow context,
without claiming long-term or cross-context validity.

Exploratory Notes may inform Applied Notes,
but do not constitute them.

---

## Non-Goals

Applied Notes are not:

- best-practice guidelines  
- decision recipes  
- optimization frameworks  
- normative recommendations  
- general methods  

If an Applied Note is treated as any of the above,
it is being used outside its intended scope.

---

## Publication Rule

Applied Notes are not published by default.

They are introduced publicly only when:

- a concrete application context is explicit and necessary  
- meta-level principles are already established and accessible  
- readers explicitly seek translation into practice  

Until then, Applied Notes remain internal.

---

## Core Distinction

> Working Notes define **what must be understood first**.  
> Applied Notes define **what may work here**.  
> Exploratory Notes define **what is still being thought through**.

---

## Versioning

Applied Notes use versioning to indicate **context stability**, not progress.

Version changes reflect:
- changes in assumptions  
- changes in scope  
- changes in applicability  

Minor revisions without scope change do not require reversioning.
