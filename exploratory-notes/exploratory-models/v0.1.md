# Exploratory Models  
### On Visible Thinking and the Limits of Model Typing

**Model type:** Exploratory meta model<br>
**Scope:** Model development, epistemic states, and visible thinking  
**Status:** Stable clarification  
**Version:** v0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-21

---

## Note

**Exploratory Models** are not model types, but **visible states of thinking**.

They are used to articulate structure and meaning while a model is still forming.
Exploratory Models are intentionally unstable, incomplete, and **not intended for direct application**.

In contrast to the models described in *Typing of Models*, Exploratory Models carry **no claim of stability or general validity**. They are therefore **not typified, not canonized, and not treated as reusable forms**.

They exist here solely to make **thinking in motion** visible, not to provide finished knowledge.

> Exploratory Models show thinking.  
> Typed models carry knowledge.

---

## Relation to Typing of Models

*Typing of Models* describes **stable model forms** that can be used independently of their author.

Exploratory Models deliberately fall outside this classification.
They represent a **developmental state**, not a model category.

Not everything that is visible must be typified.

## Status

This note is a **clarification**, not a framework.

It exists to prevent category errors and misinterpretation of exploratory material and is **not intended to be cited or used as a stable reference**.
