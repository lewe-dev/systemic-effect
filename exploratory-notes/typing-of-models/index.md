## Scope

This note addresses the **functional classification of analytical models**
(i.e. *what a model is primarily intended to do*).

It distinguishes between:

- Orientation Models
- Explanatory Models
- Application Models
- Meta Models

The typology classifies models by their primary function rather than their quality, empirical validity, or scientific status.
