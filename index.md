# Systemic Effect

The site collects models and working 
notes concerned with the structure of 
systems, the conditions under which they
operate, and how actions translate into 
effects at the system level. 

Systems define the space of possibilities. 
Conditions select from it. 
Effects are the result - and in turn 
reshape conditions and possibilities.
